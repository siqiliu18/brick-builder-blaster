How to play

  • Move with the left stick, WASD, or the arrow keys.
  • Aim and shoot with the right stick, or hold the mouse. Drag it all the way around, including diagonals. Hold the stick to keep firing, and let go to stop. The barrel or blaster points where the shots go.

Red walls break after 3 hits. Blue walls never break. The mad circle pops after 2 hits.

It starts slow and gets faster. Touching it costs one heart. At 0 hearts you pop back to Start.

Reach the blue flag to clear the level.
